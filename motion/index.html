Every entry has three things: a live demo you can actually manipulate, the prompt that generates it, and the React code it produces. Copy either one straight into your project.

Everything runs on one closed-form spring solver and one cubic-bezier solver — no animation library, no build step. Springs carry velocity across interruptions, which is why a drawer you throw keeps its momentum into the snap instead of restarting.
